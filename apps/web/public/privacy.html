Privacy

Hero Table 3D, https://herotable3d.netlify.app. Date of this policy: 2026-10-11.

Who runs the app

The app is run by its developer, a private person.

To reach them, write to pompolutz@gmail.com or message them on Discord: fxdxpz#5790.

What the app stores, and why

After a login with Discord, the app stores:

  • Your Discord id, username, display name and avatar.

The app uses them to show your name and avatar in the lobby and to your opponent, and to keep the seats of a room. The app asks Discord for the scope identify only. It does not get your email, your servers or your friends.

For multiplayer rooms, the server stores:

  • The map, the rosters and the seats.
  • The game table.
  • The dates of the room.

Two browsers connect with short messages that go through the server. They contain the network address (IP address) of the browser. The server deletes them after 1 hour.

The server keeps technical logs. Each request leaves one line: the method, the path (with the room code), the status and the time. The logs have no game content and no login tokens. The server deletes them after 1 week.

When the app has an error, the browser sends a short report to the server: the error text, where in the code it happened, the app version, your browser name and version, and the room code and your seat when you are in a multiplayer room. It has no account data, and the server does not store your IP address. Reports are kept in a small list and the oldest are deleted first.

Your browser keeps the login token, your single player rooms, and a copy of each multiplayer table. The login token goes only to the server of the app, with each request. Single player rooms stay in the browser. The copy of a multiplayer table syncs with the server and with your opponent.

Who gets data

  • Your opponent gets your name and your avatar. During a game, the two browsers connect directly. So each browser gets the IP address of the other one.
  • Netlify hosts the site. It sees the IP address of each visit.
  • Amazon Web Services runs the server, in Frankfurt.
  • MongoDB Atlas is the database. It runs on AWS in Frankfurt.
  • Discord does the login. Avatars load from the servers of Discord.
  • Google and Cloudflare run STUN servers. They see your IP address when two browsers connect.

The app has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no cookies. The browser storage holds only what the app needs.

All data on the server stays in the EU (Frankfurt).

How long

  • An account with no login and no app start for 12 months is deleted.
  • A room with no change for 12 months is deleted, with its table.
  • Connection messages are deleted after 1 hour.
  • Server logs are deleted after 1 week.
  • Error reports are deleted when the list is full (about 10 MB), the oldest first.

Your choices

  • Delete account is in the user menu of the lobby. It deletes your account and the room that you host, with its table, at once. In rooms of other hosts, your seat shows "Deleted player".
  • You can remove the app in Discord: User Settings → Authorized Apps.
  • For a copy of your data, a correction, or any other question, write to the contact above.
  • You have the right to complain to the data protection authority of your country.
